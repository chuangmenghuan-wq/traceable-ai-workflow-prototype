# Claim Boundaries

This competition package intentionally separates demonstrated controls from planned competition-specific work.

## Demonstrated / bounded evidence

- deterministic AUTO / APPROVAL / DENY authority pattern in a sandbox recovery system;
- independent business probes separated from execution success;
- a deterministic fault-injection scenario where execution succeeds while required business probes fail and completion is blocked;
- evidence-driven replan where the next legal candidate depends on new evidence;
- a fault matrix designed to prevent false success and unauthorised AUTO actions.

## Competition-specific work

- clean four-scene Autonomous SRE demonstration;
- model/runtime/hardware source swap with requalification;
- deployment of the selected executor on ASUS UGen Intel Arc Pro B70 32GB;
- final English demonstration video.

## Explicitly not claimed as completed

- 72-hour autonomous operation PASS;
- full Autonomous SRE Recovery Root PASS;
- Intel Arc Pro B70 throughput/latency measurements before hardware runs;
- production-enterprise reliability from sandbox test counts.

This boundary is part of the product philosophy: evidence should be strong enough to support the exact claim being made, and no stronger.
